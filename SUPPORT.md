# 支持与反馈 / Support

先查看[使用指南](docs/USER_GUIDE.md)、[安装指南](docs/INSTALLATION.md)和[已知限制](README.md#当前边界)。在 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/JDEgg-Bing/Folio/issues/new/choose) 选择错误报告或功能建议；应用内“帮助 → 问题反馈”可复制记录模板。

错误报告提供版本、Windows 版本、复现步骤、预期和实际结果。截图、文稿和日志先脱敏，尽量使用最小示例。功能建议说明写作场景与现有困难。无需上传整个个人资料目录。

社区支持不承诺固定响应时间。安全问题按[安全政策](SECURITY.md)私密报告；行为投诉见[行为准则](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)。

## English

Check the [user guide (中文)](docs/USER_GUIDE.md), [installation guide](docs/INSTALLATION.md) and [known limits](README.en.md#current-boundaries). Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/JDEgg-Bing/Folio/issues/new/choose). In-app Help → Feedback can copy a report template.

Include application and Windows versions, steps, expected behavior and actual results. Share minimal, anonymized examples; do not upload your whole data directory. Describe the writing task and difficulty for feature requests. Support is best-effort. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities and [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for conduct reports.
